Pre-mortem Red Team — Notewise launch

A fictional 3-person team launches Notewise, an AI note-taking app for independent consultants, via founder-led LinkedIn content and live demos. Goal: 500 paying users in 12 months.

Decision: Commit a 6-month runway and a Q3 public launch on a LinkedIn-only acquisition bet.   Horizon: 12 months post-launch

Load-bearing assumptions

  • A1: LinkedIn founder content will drive 60% of signups at a 3% visitor-to-trial rate. load-bearing
  • A2: Consultants will pay $20/mo once they try the product for a week. load-bearing
  • A3: The team can ship a reliable v1 by the Q3 date with current headcount. load-bearing
  • A4: Incumbent note apps will not ship a comparable AI feature within the year.

Residual risk (accepted knowingly)

Even with mitigations, the plan still rests on a single founder's content output as the primary growth engine for the first two quarters. If the founder is unavailable for an extended period, no mitigation fully covers the gap; the team accepts this knowingly and revisits at the 6-month mark.
